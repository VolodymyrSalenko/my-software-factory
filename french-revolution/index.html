Why did it happen?

Three pressures built up before 1789.

Unequal society

Clergy and nobles had privileges. Almost everyone else, the Third Estate, paid most of the taxes.

Empty treasury

Wars and court spending left the crown deep in debt. Roughly half of the royal budget went on interest.

Hungry people

Bad harvests pushed bread prices up. In 1789 a loaf cost far more of a worker's day than usual.

Three Estates, one vote each

At the Estates-General of 1789, how votes were counted decided everything.
